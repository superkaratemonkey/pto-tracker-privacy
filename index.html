Privacy policy

PTO Tracker is a personal app for tracking paid time off. This policy explains what information the app handles. The short version: your information stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We do not collect it, see it, sell it or share it.

Information the app stores

The days you mark (days off, PTO, early outs and unpaid hours) and your settings (start date, balance, hire date or years of service, accrual rates, pay-period details, days off, colours and appearance) are stored on your device. There are no accounts, and nothing is sent to the developer.

iCloud backup (optional)

If you turn on "Back up to iCloud", your days and settings are saved to your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so they can restore on your other devices and after a reinstall. This data is held by Apple under your Apple ID and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot access it.

Calendar access (optional)

If you choose "Add to my calendar", the app asks for permission to use your calendar. It then adds and updates all-day events for the days you select, and removes only events it created. Calendar events are written to the calendar you choose, including a Google calendar if that account is added to your device. The app does not read or upload your other calendar events.

Notifications (optional)

If you turn on reminders, the app asks for notification permission and schedules reminders locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Widget

The Home Screen widget shows your PTO balance. It reads a small summary shared between the app and the widget on your device only.

Feedback emails

If you tap "Send feedback", your email app opens a message addressed to the developer. It includes the app and system version. Nothing is sent unless you press send, and we use your email only to respond to you.

Analytics, tracking and third parties

PTO Tracker contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party services. If you test through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect crash reports and feedback you choose to submit, under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your control

You can delete your data at any time by deleting the app. To remove iCloud copies, turn off iCloud for the app in iOS Settings or manage storage in your iCloud settings. To remove calendar events, use "Remove from calendar" in the app before deleting it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
